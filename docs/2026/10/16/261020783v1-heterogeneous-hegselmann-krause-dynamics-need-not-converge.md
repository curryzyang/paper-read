## TLDR
The paper disproves the conjecture that scalar heterogeneous Hegselmann–Krause dynamics always converge by giving an explicit six-agent example whose trajectory is periodic with period ten.

## Abstract
We give a six-agent counterexample to convergence of scalar heterogeneous Hegselmann--Krause dynamics.
