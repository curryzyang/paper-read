## TLDR
The paper introduces AllotNet, an interpretable mixture-of-experts conditional-logit model for multicultural moral judgments in humanitarian aid allocation that builds a human-review queue rather than rejecting anyone, validated against a pre-specified planted synthetic population of 5.6 million sessions where all 16 checks passed, though it has not yet been trained on real data.

## Abstract
Official development assistance fell by 23.1 per cent in 2025, and the decision of who is helped first when there is not enough for everyone is moving into software. A leading published machine-learning targeting system excluded 53 per cent of eligible households as deployed. If the rule of distribution ends up in code, it should carry the judgement of many cultures, and the machine should execute while a person judges. AllotNet is an interpretable mixture-of-experts conditional logit for the allocation judgements elicited by Allot, a pre-registered six-language instrument: eight linear taste experts over seven ethical criteria, weighted by a gate over participant context, with a damped residual, an encoder for household intake, an outcome head and a budget-constrained planner that builds a review queue and never rejects anyone. A model of human judgement has no answer key on real data, so we wrote one first: a planted synthetic population of 5.6 million sessions and 104 million responses (98 million usable choices), generated by the production code path from a ground truth hidden from the loss. All sixteen pre-specified checks passed: criterion ranking $ρ= 1.000$, hidden segments recovered (ARI 0.671), outcome head at 99 per cent of the noise ceiling, planner at 99.4 per cent of oracle. The caveats are reported with them: two checks are weaker than their titles, and a live-generation failure cost 260,712 seeds. The model has 23,550 parameters, is trained quantisation-aware for NVFP4 with its interpretable core in fp32 (welfare-rank parity $ρ= 0.9937$ at W4A16 on the shipped bundle; one parity threshold was relaxed post hoc), and ships as a 125 KB bundle with C11, Java, Rust and Python runtimes. Nothing has been trained on real data yet; at the study's real scale a hierarchical Bayesian mixed logit is the registered comparator.
