## TLDR
The paper introduces ACCESS, a structured sampling framework for chance-constrained stochastic programs that decouples optimization from feasibility by generating candidates from inexpensive single-scenario subproblems and evaluating feasibility via batched simulation, reducing the search to a two-dimensional conservatism–diversity space and achieving near-best objectives with strong or certified feasibility guarantees.

## Abstract
Chance-constrained stochastic programs (CCSPs) require solutions that satisfy uncertain constraints with high probability. Standard approaches either embed scenarios directly into optimization by considering sample average approximation (SAA)-based reformulations, growing prohibitively expensive and often failing to find feasible solutions; or employ conservative reformulations that guarantee feasibility but sacrifice objective quality by design. We propose ACCESS (Adaptive Chance-Constrained Exploration via Structured Sampling), a structured sampling framework that decouples optimization from feasibility evaluation. First, high-quality candidate solutions are generated by solving many inexpensive single-scenario subproblems, avoiding sampling directly in the constrained high-dimensional solution space. Second, feasibility is assessed separately via large-scale batched simulation over a massive number of scenarios, which is naturally amenable to GPU parallelism. That reduces the search from a high-dimensional solution space to a two-dimensional parameter space controlling conservatism and scenario diversity; while concentrating on candidates near the feasibility boundary, where high-quality feasible solutions lie. We also introduce a variant, ACCESS($δ$), which provides certified feasibility guarantees with probability $1-δ$. Experiments across three benchmark classes and five scenario distributions show that ACCESS matches or exceeds the best objective found by any method in nearly every setting, while ACCESS($δ$) is empirically feasible on every instance. The conservative reformulations remain several percent worse in objective, and SAA is frequently infeasible while taking an order of magnitude longer on the harder benchmarks.


## 精读解读（中文）
### 一、研究动机
机会约束随机规划要求解以高概率满足不确定约束，但现有主流做法存在两难：基于样本平均近似（SAA）的重构把场景直接嵌入优化，规模增长导致计算昂贵且在困难实例上常常找不到可行解；而保守重构虽能保证可行性，却以目标质量为代价。因此需要一种把优化与可行性评估解耦的框架，在可接受的预算下同时兼顾目标质量与可行性。

### 二、技术方案（Method）
本文提出 ACCESS（Adaptive Chance-Constrained Exploration via Structured Sampling）：首先通过求解大量廉价的单场景子问题生成高质量候选解，从而避免在受约束的高维解空间中直接采样；随后将可行性评估与优化分离，用海量场景的大规模批量仿真评估候选解，这种评估天然适合 GPU 并行。该设计把搜索从高维解空间降为控制保守性与场景多样性的二维参数空间，并让搜索聚焦于可行域边界附近，即高质量可行解所在区域。作者还给出变体 ACCESS(δ)，在给定 δ 时提供概率 1−δ 的认证可行性保证。整体推理流程为：批量求解单场景子问题得到候选集，批量仿真评估可行性，在二维参数空间自适应调整后输出最终解。

### 三、结果（Result）
在三个基准类别与五种场景分布的实验中，ACCESS 在几乎所有设置下匹配或超过任一对比方法所能找到的最佳目标值；其变体 ACCESS(δ) 在每一个实例上都经验可行。保守重构方法的目标值仍差几个百分点，而 SAA 在较难的基准上频繁不可行，并且耗时高出一个数量级。

### 四、结论（Conclusion）
将优化与可行性评估解耦、并在低维参数空间中搜索可行域边界附近候选解的思路，能够在不牺牲可行性的前提下取得与最强基线相当甚至更优的目标质量。ACCESS(δ) 进一步以概率 1−δ 提供可行性证书，使该方法在保证性与效率上都优于 SAA 与保守重构两类经典方案。

### 五、方法论与关键技术细节
关键实现点包括：用大量廉价单场景子问题替代受约束高维解空间采样来产生候选；可行性通过海量场景的批量仿真在 GPU 上并行评估；搜索空间被压缩为保守性与场景多样性两个参数；策略围绕可行性边界进行自适应集中采样；ACCESS(δ) 通过 δ 控制可行性证书的概率水平。评测覆盖三类基准与五种场景分布，并与 SAA 和保守重构对比目标值、可行率与运行时间。局限在于 ACCESS(δ) 的可行性在实验中为经验验证而非全部理论证明，且性能依赖仿真场景规模与批量评估预算。
