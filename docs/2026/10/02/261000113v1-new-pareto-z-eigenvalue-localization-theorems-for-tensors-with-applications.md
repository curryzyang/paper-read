## TLDR
This paper establishes new, sharper Pareto Z-eigenvalue localization sets for tensors, compares them with existing sets, and uses them to derive sufficient conditions for the solvability of tensor complementarity and tensor generalized eigenvalue complementarity problems and to test vacuum stability of a general scalar potential.

## Abstract
In this paper, we establish some new localization sets for Pareto Z-eigenvalues of tensor eigenvalue complementarity problems and discuss the relationships among existing ones. As applications, a sufficient condition for the solution existence of the tensor complementarity problem (TCP) and the tensor generalized eigenvalue complementarity problem (TGEiCP) are presented. In addition, this condition is also used to test the vacuum stability of a general scalar potential
