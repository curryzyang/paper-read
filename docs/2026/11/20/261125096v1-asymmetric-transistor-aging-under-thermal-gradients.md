## TLDR
This paper shows that realistic 10–15°C on-chip thermal gradients cause asymmetric BTI-induced transistor aging in 16 nm FinFET circuits, producing setup and hold timing violations that conventional uniform-temperature signoff misses, so thermal-gradient-aware aging analysis must be included in timing signoff.

## Abstract
Advanced technology nodes improve performance but increase susceptibility to BTI-induced aging. Using aging- aware circuit simulations, we show that realistic on-chip thermal gradients can cause significant asymmetric transistor degradation in 16 nm FinFET circuits, leading to setup and hold time violations. These results highlight the necessity of incorporating thermal-gradient-aware aging analysis into timing signoff to ensure chip reliability.
