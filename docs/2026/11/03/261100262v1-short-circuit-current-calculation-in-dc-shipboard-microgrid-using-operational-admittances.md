## TLDR
The paper proposes an operational admittance modeling and superposition-based method to accurately and efficiently calculate pole-to-pole short-circuit current transients in LVDC shipboard microgrids for fault analysis and protection coordination, validated against a detailed EMT model.

## Abstract
The increasing adoption of DC shipboard power systems is driven by the need to integrate energy storage systems seamlessly onboard. In this context, accurate calculation of short-circuit current transients is critical for sizing fast-acting protection devices. This paper proposes a methodology to calculate short-circuit current transients based on operational admittance modelling of network elements and power converters. The superposition principle is then applied to calculate current contributions and voltage time profiles. The methodology is validated with a detailed EMT model of a notional LVDC shipboard microgrid section consisting of an active front end, a bidirectional buck-boost converter and two special loads. Results demonstrate that the methodology accurately tracks nodal voltages and the fault current contributions dynamics up to the prospective fault current peak, thus providing a computationally efficient tool for fault analysis and protection coordination in LVDC microgrids.
