## Abstract
Characterizing data center-grid interactions under rapid load fluctuations requires electromagnetic transient (EMT) models that capture fast converter and control dynamics. This paper develops a modular multi-fidelity EMT model of data center loads in PSCAD. The modular design supports flexible assembly of subsystem models for different power supply configurations. The multi-fidelity design incorporates switching models, average-converter models, and reduced-order models to balance simulation accuracy and computational efficiency according to analysis needs. The model also provides a systematic controller tuning procedure and supports control mode switching. Comparative simulations demonstrate how this data center load model responds to both grid-side and load-side disturbances and assess the accuracy and computational efficiency of models with three fidelity levels. The model is publicly available on GitHub.
