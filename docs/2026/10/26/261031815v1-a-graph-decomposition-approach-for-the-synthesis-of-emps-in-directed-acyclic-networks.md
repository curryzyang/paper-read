## TLDR
This paper proposes a DAG decomposition into trees and parallel-path networks to synthesize a parsimonious valid Excitation and Measurement Pattern that guarantees identifiability of the entire dynamical network with few excited and measured nodes.

## Abstract
This paper considers the identifiability of dynamical networks whose topology is a Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG), in the context where not all nodes are excited and not all nodes are measured. The objective is to construct a valid and parsimonious
  Excitation and Measurement Pattern (EMP), i.e. a selection of excited and measured nodes, that allows to identify the whole network with a small number of excitations and measurements. We show first that a DAG can be decomposed into a set of trees and parallel paths networks. We propose an algorithm that achieves this decomposition and computes an EMP that combines EMPs for the individual trees and parallel paths networks.
  We formally show that this algorithm yields an EMP capable of identifying the whole network.
